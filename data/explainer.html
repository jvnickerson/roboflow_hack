01

What you're building: three channels on one switcher

Empty New York only shows street scenes with nothing in them — a person or (since 7:30) a vehicle entering the frame cuts it away to another empty scene. Noir New York, added at 8 PM, qualifies scenes on contrast instead. Water on the Lens, born as the demo fallback, finds rain on camera glass. The page's buttons and 1/2/3 keys switch channels, and the montage view overlaps lenses on any of them.
